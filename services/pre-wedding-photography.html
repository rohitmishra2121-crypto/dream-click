Top Pre-Wedding Shoot Locations in Delhi NCR

🏛️

Lodhi Garden

Historic Mughal-era tombs surrounded by lush greenery — perfect for a romantic, heritage-style shoot with beautiful golden hour light.

🏰

Hauz Khas Fort

Rustic medieval ruins with a serene lake backdrop — ideal for couples who want a vintage, artistic feel in their photos.

🇮🇳

India Gate

Delhi's most iconic landmark at sunrise or during evening illumination — grand, patriotic, and perfect for dramatic frames.

🌳

Garden of Five Senses

Artistic sculptures, colorful pathways, and diverse landscapes within a single venue — versatile location for varied looks.

🏗️

Kingdom of Dreams

Bollywood-themed fantasy venue in Gurgaon — vibrant, theatrical sets perfect for dramatic, fun-filled shoots.

🌅

Surajkund

Serene lakeside setting with ancient architecture in Faridabad — peaceful, natural beauty ideal for intimate couple portraits.

🏜️

Neemrana Fort

Stunning 15th century hill fort near Delhi — royalty, heritage, and breathtaking views for a truly regal pre-wedding shoot.
